VBIT Campus Navigation is a web-based campus navigation project designed to help students, faculty, and visitors find locations around the VBIT campus more conveniently.

ProblemFinding classrooms, departments, laboratories, offices, and other facilities can be difficult for people unfamiliar with campus.
ObjectiveProvide an accessible digital navigation experience that helps users identify destinations and navigate more easily.
Potential benefits
  • Helps new students and visitors become familiar with campus.
  • Makes campus destinations easier to locate.
  • Provides convenient access through a web browser.
